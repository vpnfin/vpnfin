# VPNFin

VPNFin is an independent team of digital privacy enthusiasts and cybersecurity researchers based in Southeast Asia. The platform provides transparent, rigorous, and hands-on testing of Virtual Private Network (VPN) providers, moving away from standard marketing claims to deliver genuine, data-backed reviews.

## Our Mission
Founded by long-term VPN power users, VPNFin is dedicated to helping individuals protect their online anonymity, circumvent censorship, and audit data security policies through objective, real-world testing.
